## ITC Ltd. (ITC) — Market Report (as of 04-Sep-2026)

As of early September 2026, ITC trades around **₹263–₹267**, recovering from a **52-week low ~₹255.5** but still well below the **52-week high ~₹427**, indicating a market that is broadly optimistic about ITC’s long-run diversified franchise while remaining cautious about **near-term earnings volatility**. Overall, ITC’s investment case (based on the provided research) hinges on a balancing act: **stable moats and growth engines** versus **policy/tax sensitivity** that can repeatedly affect profitability and valuations.
