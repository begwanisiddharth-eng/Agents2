# Veg Margherita Pizza (Veggie Margherita)

Source recipe: **“Margherita Pizza Recipe (Easy & Homemade)”** — Dassana’s Veg Recipes (Dassana Amit), updated **Apr 14, 2025**: https://www.vegrecipesofindia.com/margherita-pizza-recipe/

## What makes it Margherita
Thin/quick-leavened crust topped with **pizza sauce (tomato)**, **fresh mozzarella (chunks or shredded from a block)**, **fresh basil leaves**, and a **drizzle of extra-virgin olive oil** (parmesan is optional but used in this version).

## Ingredients (high-level)
### Dough
- Instant yeast, sugar, warm water (for activation if needed)
- Whole wheat flour, salt
- Olive oil
- Lemon juice (or apple cider vinegar) for acidity

### Sauce & toppings
- Pizza sauce (homemade or store-bought)
- Fresh mozzarella
- Parmesan (optional; used generously in this recipe)
- Fresh basil leaves (added after baking)
- Olive oil (drizzled after baking)
- Optional: oregano and/or red chili flakes

## Method (summary)
1. **Make dough:** Mix yeast + sugar (and warm water if using active yeast), then combine with remaining dough ingredients and knead until smooth.
2. **Proof:** Cover and let rise until roughly doubles (about **45 min to 1.5 hours**, depending on room temperature).
3. **Preheat oven hard:** Set oven to its highest setting (the recipe uses **250°C / 475°F**) and preheat **15–20 min**.
4. **Prepare crusts:** Divide dough into **6–7** portions (~9-inch thin crusts). Roll each to a thin round (about **0.3–0.5 cm**).
5. **Assemble:** Place on a lightly greased/floured (or cornmeal/semolina-dusted) tray/pan. Spread a thin layer of sauce, leaving room at the edges.
6. **Cheese:** Sprinkle **parmesan** (optional) and then add lots of **mozzarella** in chunks or shredded.
7. **Bake:** Bake on the **lowest rack position** for **10–15 minutes**, until cheese is melted and crust lightly golden.
8. **Finish:** Add **torn fresh basil** only after baking. Drizzle with **extra-virgin olive oil**. Slice and serve.

## Key tips
- Don’t bake basil before: it wilts and loses aroma.
- If mozzarella is watery (fresh), **squeeze out excess water** to avoid a soggy crust.
- Use a **block of mozzarella** and shred/grate yourself for better melting.
- This recipe yields **6–7 thin crust pizzas**.
